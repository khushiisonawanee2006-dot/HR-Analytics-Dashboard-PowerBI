# HR-Analytics-Dashboard-PowerBI
## 📌 Project Overview
This project is an interactive HR Analytics Dashboard built in Power BI using the IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition & Performance dataset. The dashboard helps analyze employee attrition and identify workforce trends through interactive visualizations.

## 🛠 Tools Used
- Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- IBM HR Analytics Dataset

## 📊 Dashboard Features
- Total Employees
- Attrition Count
- Attrition Rate
- Average Age
- Average Salary
- Average Years at Company
- Attrition by Education
- Attrition by Job Role
- Attrition by Department
- Attrition by Gender
- Attrition by Salary Slab
- Attrition by Years at Company
- Interactive Department Filters

## 💡 Key Insights
- Research & Development has the highest employee attrition.
- Laboratory Technicians have the highest attrition among all job roles.
- Most employees leave within their first few years at the company.
- Employees in lower salary slabs have higher attrition.
